01 — Foundations

A studio that treats
every release like an heirloom.

Lamka Labs Studio sits between laboratory and atelier: experimental in method, exacting in finish. The identity had to hold that tension — precise geometry, warm materials, nothing decorative that isn't also structural. It must survive a 16-pixel favicon and a ten-metre billboard with equal authority.

Position

Quiet luxury for a working studio. Restraint is the flex; the mark does the talking.

Audience

Founders, collectors, and collaborators who buy craft and keep packaging.

Voice

Short sentences. Measured confidence. One gold accent per composition, never two.

02 — The Five Directions

Five marks.
One discipline.

Each direction is built on a single idea, drawn to a strict grid, and tested at favicon scale before anything else. All five share the same palette, wordmark, and spacing system, so any of them can carry the full program.

ITERATION 01

Aperture

A lens ring frames a chamfered L, closed by a single gold period. The full stop is the signature: work leaves the studio finished. Reads instantly at 16px, embosses cleanly on paper, and cuts into metal without thin strokes to fail.

ITERATION 02

Meridian Key

One continuous gold line spirals inward and stops, deliberate, at the centre. It borrows the Greek meander, a pattern that has meant continuity for twenty-five centuries, and re-tasks it as a lab key: the path in, the problem opened. Drawn in a single stroke weight, it silkscreens and laser-etches perfectly.

ITERATION 03

Crucible

The vessel of the lab, reduced to six strokes. The gold meniscus marks the fill line: ideas poured in, work poured out, and the level always visible. The most literal of the five, and the warmest; it wants to live on glass, bottles, and packaging.

ITERATION 04

Strata

Three foundation courses, each shorter than the last, the final one gold. Craft built in layers, the way the studio actually works: research, structure, finish. The boldest and most architectural; it scales to signage larger than any other direction here.

ITERATION 05

North Star Seal

An eight-point compass star set in a fine ring: direction, craft, permanence. The most ceremonial of the five. It is a seal before it is a logo, which makes it the natural wax stamp, foil block, and app-icon mark. Heritage without nostalgia.

04 — Recommendation

The verdict.

Primary mark — recommended

01 · Aperture

The only direction that is equally strong at 16 pixels and 16 metres. The gold period gives it a signature no competitor can borrow without looking like they did. It carries the favicon, the app icon, the card, the wall.

Ceremonial seal — supporting mark

05 · North Star Seal

Paired with 01 as the seal of the house: wax stamps, foil closures, packaging stickers, and the app icon where a roundel reads better than a letterform. The two marks share one geometry and one palette, so the system stays single.
