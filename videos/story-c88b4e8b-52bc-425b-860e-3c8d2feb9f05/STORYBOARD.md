Title: Peekaboo Farm! Who's Hiding?

Target Audience: Toddlers (Ages 1-3)
Vibe: Bright, colorful, enthusiastic, and bouncy.

Scene 1: The Red Barn

    Visual: A bright red barn door is closed on screen. The doors wiggle slightly. On-screen text: Who is hiding?

    Audio: Upbeat, bouncy, nursery-rhyme instrumental playing in the background.

    Voiceover (Excited): "Let's play Peekaboo Farm! Who's behind the door?"

Scene 2: The First Animal

    Visual: The barn doors swing wide open! A happy cartoon Dog pops out, wagging its tail. On-screen text: DOG! 🐶

    Audio: Pop! sound effect.

    Voiceover: "Peekaboo! It's a Dog! What does the dog say? Woof woof!"

Scene 3: The Shaking Bush

    Visual: Cut to a big, green, leafy bush shaking slightly. On-screen text: Who's there?

    Audio: Rustling leaves sound effect.

    Voiceover: "Who is hiding in the bush?"

Scene 4: The Second Animal

    Visual: The bush parts. A cute yellow Duck waddles out, flapping its wings. On-screen text: DUCK! 🦆

    Audio: Slide whistle up! sound effect.

    Voiceover: "Peekaboo! It's a Duck! Quack quack quack!"

Scene 5: The Haybale

    Visual: Cut to a giant yellow haybale wiggling back and forth. On-screen text: One more!

    Audio: Upbeat music continues.

    Voiceover: "One more! Who is behind the hay?"

Scene 6: The Third Animal

    Visual: A black and white Cow pops its head up from behind the haybale. On-screen text: COW! 🐮

    Audio: Boing! sound effect.

    Voiceover: "It's a Cow! Mooooo!"

Scene 7: The Grand Finale

    Visual: All three animals (Dog, Duck, Cow) are on screen together, dancing or jumping. On-screen text: Great job! Subscribe!

    Audio: Music crescendos and ends happily.

    Voiceover: "Great job! See you next time! Bye-bye!"
