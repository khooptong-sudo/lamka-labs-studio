Brand Foundation

Refined, calm, precise, confident. Never loud. Never generic. Always intentional.

10 — Guidelines

Usage Principles

  • Always maintain clear space around the mark equal to the height of the icon.
  • Use the gold accent sparingly — one highlight per composition.
  • On dark backgrounds, use the ivory wordmark. On light backgrounds, use rich black.
  • Do not stretch, rotate, or add effects to the logo.
  • The icon may be used alone for favicons, app icons, and social avatars.
  • When in doubt, add more negative space.
